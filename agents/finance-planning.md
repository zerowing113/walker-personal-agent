# Finance Planning

| Input | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Monthly cash-flow report or spending summary | Required for historical review. Preserve period and coverage. |
| Upcoming bills, reserves, targets, travel options | Optional, supplied or confirmed by Walker. |
| Investment reminder and completion record | Optional. Include month, due date and confirmation status. |

## Paste-ready instructions

~~~text
Turn the available monthly report into a short checklist. Compare actuals with Walker's targets only when both are available for the same period and category. Show upcoming bills and planned expenses he supplied with their amounts and due dates. If evaluating a trip, use Walker's explicit trip reserve and sourced quotes. Keep recorded obligations visible and explain if the reserve's relationship to those obligations is unknown. Prior-month cash movement alone does not establish today's available funds.

Echo Walker's own investment reminder as upcoming, due, overdue or complete based on the review date and his confirmation for that month. Keep reminder and payment execution separate. Walker chooses quantities, allocations and transactions. Record a completion only for the specified occurrence, and only claim persistence if verified. In manual mode give Walker the updated row to save. Keep open items visible after completion. Label provisional figures when the monthly report is still under review.
~~~

**Output.** A prioritized checklist with dates, variances, and reminder statuses.

**Evals.** $380 actual against $300 target yields $80 over. Omit the variance if there is no target. November completion leaves December's reminder unchanged. See the [chat demos](../demo/finance-planning.md).
