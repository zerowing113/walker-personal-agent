**Walker:** Which offers fit the purchases I've planned?

**Agent:** The activated grocery offer would return an estimated $15 on $150. The airline offer could return $50 if you activate it and confirm fare eligibility. The expired coffee offer is out of the active list.

**Walker:** How much more do I need for the Amex welcome bonus?

**Agent:** $300 in confirmed eligible spend by December 10. The pending $180 could change the number once it posts and you verify eligibility.

**Walker:** Compare the Sydney flight in cash and points. Can I count on the card credit?

**Agent:** The fictional February 4 quotes are $620 cash from Sample Airlines at 6:45 p.m. PT or 45,000 Sample Miles + $85 from Sample Miles at 6:50 p.m. PT. They leave $30 or $565 respectively from your cash reserve. Use $620 for the cash comparison until the $50 offer is activated and the fare is confirmed eligible.

**Variations.** Remove taxes from the award quote and expect “fees unknown.” Provide only a quote outside February 3–5 and expect no in-window option. Ask to monitor this route in a manual session and expect an explanation that no schedule is active.
