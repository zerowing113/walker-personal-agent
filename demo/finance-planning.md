# Finance Planning chat demos

Load [Finance Planning](../walker-finance/references/finance-planning.md). Paste these fictional inputs and Walker's messages.

~~~text
Review date: 2026-11-15, America/Los_Angeles. Mode: manual.
October cash-flow summary: checking increased $1,600, reconciled. Dining $380.
October dining target: $300.
Upcoming: internet $65 on November 18; travel reserve $650, confirmed by Walker as separate from upcoming bills.
Reminder November: review planned investment contribution, November 15, no completion confirmation.
Reminder December: review planned investment contribution, December 15, no completion confirmation.
~~~

**Walker:** What should I review this month?

**Agent:** Review dining, which was $80 over your October target. Internet is due November 18 for $65. Your November investment contribution review is due today. You have also set aside $650 separately for travel.

**Walker:** I made my November contribution. What's still open?

**Agent:** Mark the November reminder complete based on your confirmation. Save that update in your checklist. Dining and the internet bill are still open, and the December 15 reminder remains upcoming.

**Variations.** Remove the dining target and expect the $380 actual without a variance. Remove the travel reserve and ask what you can spend on flights. Expect a request for the amount set aside, with no implied budget from October's cash increase.
